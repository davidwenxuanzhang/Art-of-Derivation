## Functions


::::tip Function

Let $X$ and $Y$ be two sets. A **function** or **mapping** from $X$ to $Y$ assigns a unique element of $Y$ to each element of $X$, More specifically, the set of $X$ is called **domain**, and the set of $Y$ is called **codomain**.

If $f$ is a function from $X$ to $Y$, then we write:

$$
f: X\longrightarrow Y
$$

The element of $Y$ assigned to $x\in X$ is denoted by $f(x)$, and thus we may write:

$$
x\longmapsto f(x)
$$

Additionally, the element  $f(x)$ is called the **value** of $f$ at $x$, or the **image** of $x$ under $f$.

Two functions: $f:X\rightarrow Y$ and $g: X\rightarrow Y$ are equal, writted $f=g$, if:

$$
f(x)=g(x)
$$
for every $x\in X$

### Constand Fcuntion

:::tip Constand Function

Given sets $X$ and $Y$ and an element $y_1\in Y$, the constand function

$$
c_{y0}:X\longrightarrow Y
$$
is defined by:

$$
c_{y_0}(x)=y_0
$$

for every $x\in X$
:::

### Identity Function

:::tip Identity Function

For any set $X$, the identity function:

$$
\operatorname{id}_X:X\longrightarrow X
$$
is defined by
$$
\operatorname{id}_X(x)=x        
$$
for every $x\in X$
:::

## Well-defined Functions and Restrictions

:::tip Restriction

Suppose $f:X\rightarrow Y$ is a function and $A\subseteq X$. The **Restriction** of $y$ to $A$ is the function

$$
f|_{A}:A\longrightarrow Y
$$
defined by 

$$
f|_{A}(x)=f(x)
$$
 for every $x\in A$

:::

:::info Example

- The function $g: \mathbb{R}\rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ defined by:

$$
g(x)=
\begin{cases}
    \frac{1}{x}, &x\ne 0\\
    0, &x=0
\end{cases}
$$

<hr/>


- The modulus function $x\mapsto |x|$ is defined on $\mathbb{R}$ by 
$$
|x|
\begin{cases}
x,&x>0\\
-x,&x\le 0
\end{cases}
$$
:::

:::tip Inclusion Map

If $A\subseteq X$, the inclusion of $A$ into $X$ is the function

$$
i_{A}:A\longrightarrow X,\qquad i_A(a)=a
$$

In other words:

$$
i_A(a)=\operatorname{id}_{X}|_A
$$

:::
