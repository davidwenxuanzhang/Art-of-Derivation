Arc Length Parametrization

For a differentible parametrized curve r(t)\mathbf{r}(t)r(t):

r(t+ϵ)=r(t)+ϵr′(t)+\mathbf{r}(t+\epsilon)=\mathbf{r}(t)+\epsilon\mathbf{r}^{\prime}(t)+r(t+ϵ)=r(t)+ϵr′(t)+

Then what is the length of the points of the curve between r(a)\mathbf{r}(a)r(a) and r(b)\mathbf{r}(b)r(b)?

Here, we will approximate the length is via small segments; that is to say, we devide the entire curve from the interval [a,b][a,b][a,b] into:

t0=a, t1=a+Δt, …, ti=a+iΔt, …, tn=b, where Δt=b−ant_0=a,\ t_1=a+\Delta{t}, \ \dots,\ t_i=a+i\Delta{t},\ \dots, \ t_n=b,\text{ where } \Delta{t}=\frac{b-a}{n} t0​=a, t1​=a+Δt, …, ti​=a+iΔt, …, tn​=b, where Δt=nb−a​

Then, the length of the curve could approximate as:

ℓn=∑i=1n∥r(ti)−r(ti−1)∥⏟the length of ith vector\ell_n=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\underbrace{\left\|{\mathbf{r}(t_i)-\mathbf{r}(t_{i-1})}\right\|}_{\mathclap{\text{the length of }i\text{th vector}}}ℓn​=i=1∑n​the length of ith vector∥r(ti​)−r(ti−1​)∥​​

From linear approximation:

r(ti)≈r(ti−1)+r′(ti−1)Δt  ⟹  r(ti)−r(ti−1)≈r′(ti−1)Δt  ⟹  ∥r(ti)−r(ti−1)∥≈∥r′(ti−1)∥Δt\begin{align*} \mathbf{r}(t_i)&\approx \mathbf{r}(t_{i-1})+\mathbf{r}^\prime(t_{i-1})\Delta{t}\\ \implies \mathbf{r}(t_i)-\mathbf{r}(t_{i-1})&\approx \mathbf{r}^\prime(t_{i-1})\Delta{t}\\ \implies \|{\mathbf{r}(t_i)-\mathbf{r}(t_{i-1})}\|&\approx \left\|\mathbf{r}^\prime(t_{i-1})\right\|\Delta{t} \end{align*}r(ti​)⟹r(ti​)−r(ti−1​)⟹∥r(ti​)−r(ti−1​)∥​≈r(ti−1​)+r′(ti−1​)Δt≈r′(ti−1​)Δt≈∥r′(ti−1​)∥Δt​

Therefore:

ℓn≈∑i=1n∥r′(ti−1)∥Δt\ell_n\approx \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left\|\mathbf{r}^\prime(t_{i-1})\right\|\Delta{t}ℓn​≈i=1∑n​∥r′(ti−1​)∥Δt

Then:

L=lim⁡n→∞ℓn=lim⁡n→∞∑i=1n∥r(ti)−r(ti−1)∥=lim⁡n→∞∑i=1n∥r′(ti−1)∥Δt⏟left Reimann Sum of ∥r′(t)∥=∫ab∥r′(t)∥ dt\begin{align*} L&=\lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}\ell_n\\ &=\lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left\|{\mathbf{r}(t_i)-\mathbf{r}(t_{i-1})}\right\|\\ &=\lim_{n\rightarrow \infty}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\underbrace{\left\|\mathbf{r}^\prime(t_{i-1})\right\|\Delta{t}}_{\text{left Reimann Sum of }\|\mathbf{r}^{\prime}(t)\|}\\ &=\int_{a}^{b}{\|\mathbf{r}^\prime (t)\|}\ \mathrm{d}t \end{align*}L​=n→∞lim​ℓn​=n→∞lim​i=1∑n​∥r(ti​)−r(ti−1​)∥=n→∞lim​i=1∑n​left Reimann Sum of ∥r′(t)∥∥r′(ti−1​)∥Δt​​=∫ab​∥r′(t)∥ dt​

Idea
