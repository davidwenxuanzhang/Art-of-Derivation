their Dot Product is:

Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality​

Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality

Given two vectors v\mathbf{v}v and w\mathbf{w}w, Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality says:

v⋅w≤∥v∥∥w∥\mathbf{v}\cdot \mathbf{w}\le \|\mathbf{v}\| \|\mathbf{w}\|v⋅w≤∥v∥∥w∥
Derivation

Use the geometric definition:

v⋅w=∥v∥∥w∥cos⁡θ≤∥v∥∥w∥(cos⁡θ<1)\begin{align*} \mathbf{v}\cdot \mathbf{w}&=\|\mathbf{v}\|\|\mathbf{w}\|\cos{\theta}\\ &\le \|\mathbf{v}\|\|\mathbf{w}\| \qquad{(\cos{\theta}< 1)} \tag*{$\blacksquare$} \end{align*}v⋅w​=∥v∥∥w∥cosθ≤∥v∥∥w∥(cosθ<1)​■​
