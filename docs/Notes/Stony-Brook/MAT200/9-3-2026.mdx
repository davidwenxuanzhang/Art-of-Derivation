### Quantified Statement 

::::tip Quantified Statement 

<div style={{ width: 'fit-content', margin: '0 auto' }}>

| Quantifier | Symbol | Meaning |
|:---:|:---:|:-----------:|
| Universal | $\forall x$ | For every $x$ | 
| Existential | $\exists x $ | There exists an $x$ | 
| Unique Existential | $\exists! x$ | There exists exactly one $x$ | 
</div>
:::info Example

Write the following statements in symbolic form:

1. Every real number $x$ has a larger real number $y$  
      → $\forall x \in \mathbb{R},\ \exists y \in \mathbb{R}: y>x$
2. There exists exactly one real solution to $x+4=0$  
      → $\exists!x\in \mathbb{R}: x+4=0 $
3. Regardless of which real $x$ is chosen, one can find a real $y$ with $y<x$  
      → $\forall x\in \mathbb{R}, \ \exists y \in \mathbb{R}: y<x$
:::
::::

### Practice Questions

:::warning Question 1

Distinguish whether the following statement is true or false: 

$$
\big(\forall x,\ \exists y: P(x,y)\big) \iff \big(\exists y:\forall x, \ P(x,y)\big)
$$
<details>
<summary><span className="math-keyword">Derivation</span></summary>

False. A counterexample:
$$
\exists y\in \mathbb{R}: \forall x\in \mathbb{R}, \ y<x 
$$
is false, where:
$$
\forall x \in\mathbb{R},\ \exists y \in\mathbb{R}: y<x
$$
is true, therefore:
$$
\big(\forall x,\ \exists y: P(x,y)\big) \not\iff \big(\exists y:\forall x, \ P(x,y)\big) \tag*{$\blacksquare$}
$$
</details>
:::

Negate: $\forall x\in \mathbb{R},\ \exists!y\in \mathbb{R}: x+y=0$

:::note Tip
To negate a unique-existential ($\exists!$) statement, there are two cases: no solution, or more than one solution.
:::
<details>
<summary><span className="math-keyword">Derivation</span></summary>

Using the [Negation Rules](./9-1-2026.mdx#Negating-Quantifier):

$$
\begin{align*}
          &\exists x\in \mathbb{R}: \neg (\exists!y\in \mathbb{R}: x+y=0)\\
    \equiv&\exists x\in \mathbb{R}:\big((\forall y\in \mathbb{R},\ x+y\ne 0)\lor (\exists y,\ z\in \mathbb{R}: y\ne z\land x+y=0\land x+z=0) \big) \tag*{$\blacksquare$}
\end{align*}
$$
</details>
