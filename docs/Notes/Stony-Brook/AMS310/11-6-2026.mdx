## Acceptance Interval

Set up: suppose $\mathbb{M}(\theta_0) $ is the model for test statistics with a two-sided hypothesis $\theta=\theta_0$, and we saquire our data abd observe a test statistics $m$.

:::danger Goal

Here, we want to determine a interval $\[a,b\]$ such that the probability of observing a false negative is $\varepsilon_{-}$, and this false negative rate varies based on our goal.

:::

Recall that from quantifying error, a [False Negative](claude: give a link) rate is:
