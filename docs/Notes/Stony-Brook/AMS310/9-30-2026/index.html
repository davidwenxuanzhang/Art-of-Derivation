Variance and Standard Deviation​

  • Variance: The variance of a discrete distribution X\mathbb{X}X is a measure of variation:
E[(X−μX)2]=Var⁡(X)=σX2=∑all x(x−μX)2M[x∣X]\begin{align*} \mathbb{E}\left[(\mathbb{X}-\mu_{\mathbb{X}})^2\right]&=\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb{X})=\sigma_{\mathbb{X}}^{2}\\ &=\sum_{\text{all }x}(x-\mu_{\mathbb{X}})^2M[x\mid \mathbb{X}] \end{align*}E[(X−μX​)2]​=Var(X)=σX2​=all x∑​(x−μX​)2M[x∣X]​
  • Standard Deviation
sd⁡(X)=σX=Var⁡(X)\operatorname{sd}(\mathbb{X})=\sigma_{\mathbb{X}}=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb{X})}sd(X)=σX​=Var(X)​

Keeping the highest value of 2 dice rolls. Determine its variance:

μX=16136M[1∣X]=136,M[2∣X]=336,…,M[6∣X]=1136\mu_{\mathbb{X}}=\frac{161}{36}\quad M[1\mid \mathbb{X}]=\frac{1}{36}, M[2\mid \mathbb{X}]=\frac{3}{36},\dots,M[6\mid \mathbb{X}]=\frac{11}{36}μX​=36161​M[1∣X]=361​,M[2∣X]=363​,…,M[6∣X]=3611​

Var⁡(X)=∑all x(x−μX)2M[x∣X]=(1−16136)2(136)+⋯=25551296=1.971\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb{X})=\sum_{\text{all }x}(x-\mu_{\mathbb{X}})^2M[x\mid \mathbb{X}]=\left(1-\frac{161}{36}\right)^2\left(\frac{1}{36}\right)+\cdots=\frac{2555}{1296}=1.971Var(X)=all x∑​(x−μX​)2M[x∣X]=(1−36161​)2(361​)+⋯=12962555​=1.971sd⁡(X)=1.971=1.404\operatorname{sd}(\mathbb{X})=\sqrt{1.971}=1.404sd(X)=1.971​=1.404

General Expectations and the Shortcut Formula​

More generally, we can think of expectations in terms of functions too:

E[h(X)]=∑all xh(x)M[x∣X]\mathbb{E}\left[h(\mathbb{X})\right]=\sum_{\text{all }x}h(x)M[x\mid \mathbb{X}]E[h(X)]=all x∑​h(x)M[x∣X]
Example

From the previous example, μ\mu μ has h(x)=xh(x)=xh(x)=x, σ2\sigma^{2}σ2 has h(x)=(x−μ)2h(x)=(x-\mu)^2h(x)=(x−μ)2
