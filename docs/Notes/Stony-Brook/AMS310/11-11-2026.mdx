## Lecture Overview

## Bullshit

:::tip Bullshit

Bullshit includes language, statistical figures ($p$-value), data graphics, and other form of presentation intended to impress an audience by distracting, overwhelming, or intimidating them with a blatant disregard for truth, logical coherance, or what information is actually being converged.

:::
