Data Visualization​

Data Visualization is a form of Qualitative Reduction, and there is rarely one correct answer to a problem.

Every effective Data Visualization has a Message, and there are ways to deliver that message more clearly, such as:

  • Add a title
  • Add annotations
  • Choose an intuitive graph type
Characteristics of a Good Data Visualization
CharacteristicsDescription
ClearThe message is easy to grasp at a glance, with no clutter or ambiguity
ConciseShows only what is needed to carry the message, with no extra decoration or data
ConcreteUses specific values, units, and real quantities rather than vague or abstract shapes
ContextualSupplies what is needed to interpret it, such as labels, scale, units, reference points, and the data source
CorrectRepresents the data accurately and without distortion (honest axes, proportions, comparisons)

There are four types of message, and each has its own favored graphs:
