Statistics​

Consider how to make expectations about our data. The end goal is to get a model for the data. This combination of data and probability is statistics.

Data​

Data

Each data point can be regarded as a random variable, which means every data point we collect has an expectation, variance, distribution, and so on, with uncertainty.

Critical dichotomy:

  • A population is a vast (infinite or inexhaustible) collection of all potential observations. Colloquially, it is the distribution we expect for our data or is the "true" distribution for our data, etc.
  • A sample consists of the observations we obtain (or might obtain) through experiments; that is, as a data set, a sample is finite.

Properties​

Properties

Properties of a population are called Parameters. Analogous properties of a sample are called statistics.

Things to keep in mind:

  1. Samples always have what can be called noise, or sampling error.
  2. The sample distribution and the population distribution need not be the same.
