## The Semester-Long Theme: Reduction

In everyday life we constantly reduce and simplify things, and that idea is the central theme of this course.

:::info Example

- Stories: reduce the human condition
- Fables/Myths: reduce morality
- Law: reduces justice
- GPA: reduces "you"

:::

The problem with reduction is that it always leaves something out. It therefore has to be done with care: we must decide deliberately what to leave out and what to keep.

Probability and Statistics are tools for making sense of data and using it to make decisions. They give us quantitative ways to study reduction.

:::info Goals
Our goals as they pertain to data reduction:
1. Examine the different types of data reduction
2. Develop effective ways to visualize and communicate data
3. Quantify our expectations for the data — this is **Probability**
4. Draw inferences and conclusions from the data — this is **Statistics**
5. Recognize several common fallacies that can knock us off course
:::
