## Lecture Overview

Last time: A formalism for working with discrete random variables
- Probability mass function: $M[x\mid \mathbb{X}]=\mathbb{P}(\mathbb{X}=x\mid \mathbb{X}\sim \operatorname{distribution})$
- Expectation: $\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}]=\sum_{\text{all }x}xM[x\mid \mathbb{X}]$

Today: More on the formalism:

- More generalized expectationsl: $\mathbb{E}\left[h(\mathbb{X}) \right]j=\sum_{\text{all }x}h(x)M[m\mid \mathbb{X}]$
- Variance: $\mathbb{E}\left[(\mathbb{X}-\mu_{\mathbb{X}})^2 \right]=\sum_{\text{all }x}(x-\mu_\mathbb{X})M[m\mid \mathbb{X}]=\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}^2]-\mathbb{E}[X]^2$
- The geometric distribution: count the number of trails needed to observe a "success"
