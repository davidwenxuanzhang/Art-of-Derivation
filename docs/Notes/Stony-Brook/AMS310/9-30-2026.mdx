## Variance and Standard Deviation

- Variance: The variance of a discrete distribution $\mathbb{X}$ is a measure of variation:

$$
\begin{align*}
\mathbb{E}\left[(\mathbb{X}-\mu_{\mathbb{X}})^2\right]&=\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb{X})=\sigma_{\mathbb{X}}^{2}\\
&=\sum_{\text{all }x}(x-\mu_{\mathbb{X}})^2M[x\mid \mathbb{X}]
\end{align*}
$$

- Standard Deviation

$$
\operatorname{sd}(\mathbb{X})=\sigma_{\mathbb{X}}=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb{X})}
$$

:::info Example

Keeping the highest value of 2 dice rolls. Determine its variance:

Recall:

$$
\mu_{\mathbb{X}}=\frac{161}{36}\quad M[1\mid \mathbb{X}]=\frac{1}{36}, M[2\mid \mathbb{X}]=\frac{3}{36},\dots,M[6\mid \mathbb{X}]=\frac{11}{36}
$$
Then: 
$$
\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb{X})=\sum_{\text{all }x}(x-\mu_{\mathbb{X}})^2M[x\mid \mathbb{X}]=\left(1-\frac{161}{36}\right)^2\left(\frac{1}{36}\right)+\cdots=\frac{2555}{1296}=1.971
$$
$$
\operatorname{sd}(\mathbb{X})=\sqrt{1.971}=1.404
$$

:::

## General Expectations and the Shortcut Formula

More generally, we can think of expectations in terms of functions too:

$$
\mathbb{E}\left[h(\mathbb{X})\right]=\sum_{\text{all }x}h(x)M[x\mid \mathbb{X}]
$$

:::info Example

From the previous example, $\mu $ has $h(x)=x$, $\sigma^{2}$ has $h(x)=(x-\mu)^2$
:::


Why this is cool?

$$
\begin{align*}
    \operatorname{Var}(\mathbb{X})&=\mathbb{E}\left[(\mathbb{X}-\mu)^2 \right]=\sum_{\text{all }x}(x-\mu)^2M[x\mid \mathbb{X}]\\
    &=\sum_{\text{all }x}(x^2-2\mu x+\mu^2)M[x\mid \mathbb{X}]\\
    &=\left(\sum_{\text{all }x} x^2M[x\mid \mathbb{X}] \right)-2\mu \left(\sum_{\text{all }x }xM[x\mid \mathbb{X}] \right)+\mu^2\left(\sum_{\text{all }x }M[x\mid \mathbb{X}] \right)\\
    &=\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}^2]-2 \mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}]^2+\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}]^2\\
    &=\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}^2]-\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}]^2\\
\end{align*}
$$

:::info Example

Suppose $\mathbb{X}$ is a discrete random variable with the following probability mass function. Determine $\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}],\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}^2],\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}^3-\mathbb{X}]$

$$
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c}
x & M[x\mid \mathbb{X}] & x M[x\mid \mathbb{X}] & x^2 M[x\mid \mathbb{X}] & (x^3-x)M[x\mid \mathbb{X}]\\
\hline
0 & 0.2 & 0 & 0 & 0\\
1 & 0.7 & 0.7 & 0.7 & 0\\
2 & 0.1 & 0.2 & 0.4 & 0.6\\
\hline
\sum & 1 & \underbrace{0.9}_{\mathbb{E}[X]=\mu} & \underbrace{1.1}_{\mathbb{E}[X^2]} & \underbrace{0.6}_{\mathbb{E}[X^3-X]}
\end{array}
$$

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb{X})=\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}^2]-\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}]^2=1.1-0.9^2=1.1-0.81=0.29
$$

:::

## Binomial Distribution

:::info Binomial Distribution


Recall that $\mathbb{E}[\operatorname{binomial}(N,p)]=Np$, and let $\mathbb{X}\sim \operatorname{binomial}(N,p)$:

$$
\begin{align*}
\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}^2]&=\sum_{x=0}^{N}x^2M[x\mid \mathbb{X}]\\
&=\sum_{x=0}^{N}x^2\binom{N}{x}p^{x}(1-p)^{N-x}\\
&=N^2p^2-Np^2+Np
\end{align*}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb{X})&=\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}^2]-\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{X}]^2\\
&=N^2p^2-Np^2+Np-N^2p^2\\
&=Np-Np^2\\
&=Np(1-p)
\end{align*}
$$

:::
