## Induction

::::tip Induction

We will explain this with an example:

:::info Example
Prove: 
$$
1+2+\cdots +n=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

Let $1+2+\cdots +n=P_n$, then our Base Case :

$$
P_1=1=\frac{1(1+1)}{2}=1
$$

Induction step: Assume 

$$
1+2+\cdots +n =\frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$
Then:
$$
\implies 1+2+\cdots +n+(n+1)=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}+(n+1)=\frac{(n+1)(n+2)}{2} \tag*{$\blacksquare$}
$$

:::

### Problem 6

:::warning Problem 6

For every positive integer $n$, prove that

$$
1+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}
+\cdots+\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}
<2\sqrt{n}.
$$

<details>
<summary><span className="math-keyword">Derivation</span></summary>

Let 
$$
P_n=1+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\cdots +\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}< 2\sqrt {n}
$$
Base Case shows: 
$$
P_1=1< 2\sqrt {1}=2
$$
Induction Step: assume 
$$
P_n=1+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\cdots +\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}< 2\sqrt {n}
$$
Then: 
$$
P_{n+1}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}+\cdots +\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}}< 2\sqrt{n}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}}
$$
So we are proving: 
$$
\begin{align*}
    2\sqrt{n}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}}&< 2(\sqrt{n+1}) \\
    \implies 2\sqrt{n}&< 2(\sqrt{n+1})-\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}}\\\\
    2\sqrt{n}&< \frac{2(n+1)-1}{\sqrt{n+1}}\\
    \sqrt{n}\sqrt{n+1}&< n+\frac{1}{2}\\
    n^2+n &<n^2+n+\frac{1}{4} \qquad{(\text{square both side})}  \tag*{$\blacksquare$}
\end{align*}
$$
</details>
