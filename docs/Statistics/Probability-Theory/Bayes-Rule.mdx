## Bayes' Rule {#Bayes-Rule}

:::tip Bayes' Rule

Let event $A$ and $B$ with positive probability, then: 

$$
P(B\mid A)=\frac{P(B)P(A\mid B)}{P(A)}
$$

Applying the [Law of Total Probability](docs/Statistics/Probability-Theory/Law-of-Total-Probability.mdx#LoTP):

$$
P(B\mid A)=\frac{P(B)P(A\mid B)}{P(B)P(A\mid B)+P(B^{c})P(A\mid B^{c})}
$$

<details>
<summary><span className="math-keyword">Derivation</span></summary>

From the [multiplication rule](docs\Statistics\Probability-Theory\Conditional-Probability.mdx#multiplication-rule): 

$$
P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B\mid A)
$$

Divide $P(A)$ on both side:

$$
P(B\mid A)=\frac{P(B)P(A\mid B)}{P(A)} \tag*{$\blacksquare$}
$$
</details>

:::

Bayes' Rule is extremely important in statistics, because it transmit an idea that probability, especially conditioned, can vary based on the probability of another.
