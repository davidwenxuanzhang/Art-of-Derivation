## Law of the Unconscious Statistician

Sometimes, after transformation, the PMF might be trenuous to found; then how do we calculate the expected value? 

::::tip Law of the Unconscious Statistician (LotUS)

Let $X$ be discrete random variable with PMF $p_X(x)$, then:

$$
\mathbb{E}[g(X)]=\sum_{x}g(x)p_X{（x）}
$$
