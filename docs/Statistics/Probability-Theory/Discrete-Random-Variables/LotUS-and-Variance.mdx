## Law of the Unconscious Statistician

Sometimes, after transformation, the PMF might be trenuous to found; then how do we calculate the expected value? 

::::tip Law of the Unconscious Statistician (LotUS)

Let $X$ be discrete random variable with PMF $p_X(x)$, then:

$$
\mathbb{E}[g(X)]=\sum_{x}g(x)p_X{(x)}
$$
<details>
<summary><span className="math-keyword">Derivation</span></summary>

</details>
:::note Fun Fact
Heresay, this is called Law of the Unconscious Statistician is because initially some statisticians use this blindly or purely by instinct without any proof.
:::

::::



## Variance 

:::tip Variance For Discrete Random Variables

Let $X$ be a discrete random variable; then, the **variance** of $X$ is:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)\overset{\text{def}}= \mathbb{E}\left[(X-\mathbb{E}[X])^2\right]
$$

:::

However, this form is not neat, so we have a shortcut for variance.

:::tip Shortcut Formula for Variance

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-\left(\mathbb{E}[X]\right)^2
$$
<details>
<summary><span className="math-keyword">Derivation</span></summary>

Let $\mu=\mathbb{E}[X]$ as they both stands for the mean, which $\mu$ is also a constant, and $X$ is a discrete random variable:

$$
\begin{align*}
    \operatorname{Var}(X)&=\mathbb{E}[(X-\mu)^2]\\
                         &=\mathbb{E}[X^2-2X\mu+\mu^2]\\
                         &=\mathbb{E}{X^2}-\mathbb{E}[2X\mu]+\mathbb{E}[\mu^2]\\
                         &=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-2\mu\mathbb{E}[X]+\mu^2\\
                         &=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-2\mu^2+\mu^2\\
                         &=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-\left(\mathbb{E}[X]\right)^2 \tag*{$\blacksquare$}
\end{align*}
$$
</details>
:::


:::tip Standard Deviation of a Discrete Random Variable

Let $X$ be a discrete random variable; then, the **standard deviation** of $X$ is:

$$
\operatorname{SD}(X)\overset{\text{def}}= \sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}
$$
:::

## Additional Transformation

With LotUS, we can have a shortcut of finding expect value and variance while facing some basic transformation.

:::tip Expected Value of a Linear Transformation

Let $X$ be a random variable and $a$ and $b$ be constants, then:

$$
\mathbb{E}[aX+b]=a\mathbb{E}[X]+b
$$
<details>
<summary><span className="math-keyword">Derivation</span></summary>

Applying LotUS directly:
$$
\begin{align*}
    \mathbb{E}[aX+b]&=\sum_{x}(ax+b)p_X(x)\\
                    &=\sum_{x}ax p_X(x)+\sum_{x}(b)p_X(x)\\
                    &=a\sum_{x}x p_X(x)+b\sum_{x}p_X(x)\\
                    &=a\mathbb{E}[X]+b \tag*{$\blacksquare$}
\end{align*}
$$

</details>
:::

:::tip Variance of a Linear Transformation

Let $X$ be a random variable and $a$ and $b$ be constants, then:

$$
\operatorname{Var}[aX+b]=a^2\operatorname{Var}[X]
$$
<details>
<summary><span className="math-keyword">Derivation</span></summary>

Applying the Shortcut Formula for Variance directly:
$$
\begin{align*}
    \operatorname{Var}[aX+b]&=\mathbb{E}[(aX+b)^2]-\left(\mathbb{E}[aX+b] \right)^2\\
                    &=\mathbb{E}[a^2X^2+2abX+b^2]-\left(a\mathbb{E}[X]+b\right)^2 \\
                    &=\mathbb{E}[a^2X^2]+\mathbb{E}[2abX]+\mathbb{E}[b^2]-\left(a\mathbb{E}[X]\right)^2-2ab\mathbb{E}[X]-b^2 \\
                    &=a^2\mathbb{E}[X^2]+2ab\mathbb{E}[X]+b^2-a^2\left(\mathbb{E}[X]\right)^2-2ab\mathbb{E}[X]-b^2\\
                    &=a^2\mathbb{E}[X^2]-a^2\left(\mathbb{E}[X]\right)^2\\
                    &=a^2\operatorname{Var}[X] \tag*{$\blacksquare$}
\end{align*}
$$

</details>
:::
