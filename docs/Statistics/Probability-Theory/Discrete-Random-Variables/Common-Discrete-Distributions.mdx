##  Bernoulli Distribution

::::tip Bernoulli Distribution

If $X$ is a random variable with the PMF


$$
p_X(x) = 
\left\{\begin{aligned}
&p, &x=1\\
&1-p, &x=0
\end{aligned}
\right.
$$

for some $0\le p\le 1$, then $X$ is said to be a **Bernoulli random variable with parameter $p$**, written as $X\sim \operatorname{Bernoulli}(p)$
:::info Example
The Bernoulli distribution models a single trial with two possible outcomes, such as success or fail, true or false, yes or no.
:::


::::

:::tip Bernoulli Expectation and Variance

Let $X\sim \operatorname{Bernoulli}(p) $, then:
$$
\mathbb{E}[X]=p
$$
and 
$$
\operatorname{Var}[X]=p(1-p)
$$

## Binomial Distribution
::::tip Binomial Distribution

If $X$ is a random variable with the PMF


$$
p_X(x)=\binom{n}{x}p^x(1-p)^{n-x}; \qquad x=0,1,\dots ,n
$$

for some $n$ and $0\le p\le 1$, then $X$ is said to be a **Binomial random variable with parameters $n$ and $p$**, written as  $X\sim \operatorname{Binomial}(n, p)$

:::


::::

:::tip Binomial Expectation and Variance

Let $X\sim \operatorname{Binomial}(p) $, then:
$$
\mathbb{E}[X]=np
$$
and 
$$
\operatorname{Var}[X]=np(1-p)
$$
<details>
<summary><span className="math-keyword">Derivation</span></summary>


Given a Binomial random variable $X\sim \operatorname{Binomial}(p) $, we have the PMF: 

$$
p_X(x)=\mathbb{P}(x=k)=\binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k} 
$$
<hr />
 
- Expected Value:

Use the definition of Expected Value:

$$
\begin{align*}
    \mathbb{E}[X]&=\sum_{k}k\cdot p_X(k)\\
                 &=\sum_{k}k\binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k} \\
                 &=\sum_{k}n\binom{n-1}{k-1}p^k(1-p)^{n-k} &&(\text{“Choose a Captain” identity})\\
                 &=np\sum_{k}\binom{n-1}{k-1}p^{k-1}(1-p)^{n-k}\\
\end{align*}
$$

Let $j=k-1 \implies k=j+1,\ n-k=n-(j+1)=n-1-j$
$$
\begin{align*}
    \implies \mathbb{E}[X]&=np\sum_{j}\underbrace{\binom{n-1}{j}p^j(1-p)^{n-1-j}}_{\text{PMF of }Y \sim \operatorname{Binomial}(n-1,p)} &&\text{(PMF sum up to 1)}\\
                          &=np
\end{align*}
$$
<hr />
- Variance:

Use the definition of Variance:


$$
\operatorname{Var}[X]=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-\left(\mathbb{E}[X] \right)^2=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-(np)^2
$$
Let $g(x)=x^2$, using LotUS:

$$
\begin{align*}
    \implies \mathbb{E}[X^2]&= \sum_{k}g(k)\cdot p_X(k)\\
                            &= \sum_{k}k^2\binom{n}{k}p^{k}(1-p)^{n-k}\\
                            &= \sum_{k}kn\binom{n-1}{k-1}p^{k}(1-p)^{n-k} &&(\text{“Choose a Captain” identity})\\
                            &=np\sum_{k=1}k\binom{n-1}{k-1}p^{k-1}(1-p)^{n-k}
\end{align*}
$$
Let $j=k-1 \implies k=j+1,\ n-k=n-(j+1)=n-1-j$
$$
\begin{align*}
    \implies \mathbb{E}[X^2]&=np\sum_{j=0}(j+1)\underbrace{\binom{n-1}{j}p^{j}(1-p)^{n-1-j}}_{\text{PMF of }Y\sim \operatorname {Binomial}(n-1,p)}\\
                            &=np\left(\underbrace{\sum_{j=0}j\binom{n-1}{j}p^{j}(1-p)^{n-1-j}}_{\mathbb{E}[Y]} + \underbrace{\sum_{j=0}\binom{n-1}{j}p^{j}(1-p)^{n-1-j}}_{1} \right)\\
                            &=np\left[(n-1)p\right]+1\\
                            &=(np)^2-np^2+np
\end{align*}
$$
Plug in back to Variance:
$$
\operatorname{Var}[X]=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-(np)^2= (np)^2-np^2+np-(np)^2=np-np^2=np(1-p) \tag*{$\blacksquare$}

$$
</details>
:::

## Hypergeometric Distribution

::::tip Hypergeometric Distribution

If $X$ is a random variable with the PMF

$$
p_X(x)=\frac{\binom{M}{x}\binom{N-M}{n-x}}{\binom{N}{n}}; \qquad x=0,1,\dots ,\mathrm{min}(n,m)
$$

for positive integer $n$ and $M<N$, then $X$ is said to be a **hypergeometric random variable with parameters $n$, $M$, and $N$**, written as  $X\sim \operatorname{Binomial}(n, M, N)$
:::info Example
$M$ products defect in $N$ total, let $X$ be the random variable of defect in $n$ that pull $n$ from $N$ and $x$ are defect, without replacement.
:::

::::

:::tip Hypergeometric Expectation and Variance

Let $X\sim \operatorname{Hypergeometric}(n,M,N) $, then:
$$
\mathbb{E}[X]=n\frac{M}{N}
$$
and 
$$
\operatorname{Var}[X]=n\frac{M}{N}\left(1-\frac{M}{N}\right )\frac{N-n}{N-1}
$$
<details>
<summary><span className="math-keyword">Derivation</span></summary>


Given a Binomial random variable $X\sim \operatorname{Hypergeometric}(n,M,N) $, we have the PMF: 

$$
p_X(x)=\frac{\binom{M}{x}\binom{N-M}{n-x}}{\binom{N}{n}}
$$


Since calculating expectation and variance requires $\mathbb{E}[X]$ and $\mathbb{E}[X^2]$, we can directly <span className="math-keyword">Derive</span> a general formula for the **$k$th moment** $\mathbb{E}[X^k]$ for $k\ge 1$

$$
\begin{align*}
    \mathbb{E}[X^k]&=\sum_{x=1}^{n}x^k \frac{\binom{M}{x}\binom{N-M}{n-x}}{\binom{N}{n}} &&(\text{LotUS})\\
                   &=\sum_{x=1}^{n}x^{k-1}\frac{x\binom{M}{x}\binom{N-M}{n-x}}{\binom{N}{n}}\\
                   &=\sum_{x=1}^{n}x^{k-1}M\frac{n\binom{M-1}{x-1}\binom{N-M}{n-x}}{n\binom{N}{n}}  &&(x\binom{M}{X}=M\binom{M-1}{x-1})\\
                   &=\sum_{x=1}^{n}x^{k-1}M\frac{n\binom{M-1}{x-1}\binom{N-M}{n-x}}{N\binom{N-1}{n-1}} &&(n\binom{N}{n}=N\binom{N-1}{n-1})\\
                   &=\sum_{x=1}^{n}x^{k-1}\frac{Mn}{N}\underbrace{\frac{\binom{M-1}{x-1}\binom{N-M}{n-x}}{N\binom{N-1}{n-1}}}_{Y\sim \operatorname{Hypergeometric}(n-1,M-1,N-1)}
\end{align*}
$$
Let $i=x-1 \implies x=i+1,\ n-x=n-i-1$

$$
\begin{align*}
    \implies \mathbb{E}[X^k]&=\frac{Mn}{N}\sum_{i=0}^{n}(i+1)^{k-1}\frac{\binom{M-1}{i}\binom{N-M}{n-i-1}}{N\binom{N-1}{n-1}}\\
                            &=n\frac{M}{N}\mathbb{E}[(Y+1)^{k-1}]  &&(\text{Inverse LotUS})
\end{align*}
$$ 
where  $Y\sim \operatorname{Hypergeometric}(n-1,M-1,N-1)$
<hr />
 
- Expected Value:

When $k=1$:

$$
\begin{align*}
    \mathbb{E}[X]&=n\frac{M}{N}\mathbb{E}[(Y+1)^{1-1}]\\
                 &=n\frac{M}{N}\\
\end{align*}
$$

<hr />
- Variance:

When $k=2$:

$$
\begin{align*}
    \mathbb{E}[X^2]&=n\frac{M}{N}\mathbb{E}[(Y+1)^{2-1}]\\
                   &=n\frac{M}{N}\mathbb{E}[Y+1] \\
                   &=n\frac{M}{N}(\mathbb{E}[Y]+1) \\ 
                   &=n\frac{M}{N}\left[(n-1)\frac{M-1}{N-1}+1\right]
\end{align*}
$$

Use the definition of Variance:

$$
\begin{align*}
    \operatorname[Var][X]&=\mathbb{E}[X^2]-\left(\mathbb{E}[X] \right)^2\\
                         &=n\frac{M}{N}\left[(n-1)\frac{M-1}{N-1}+1\right]- \left(n\frac{M}{N}\right)^2\\
                         &=n\frac{M}{N}\left[(n-1)\frac{M-1}{N-1}+1-n\frac{M}{N} \right]\\
                         &=n\frac{M}{N}\left\{\frac{[(M-1)(n-1)+N-1]N-nM(N-1)}{N(N-1)} \right\}\\
                         &=n\frac{M}{N}\left[\frac{(Mn-M-n+N)N-nM(N-1)}{N(N-1)} \right]\\
                         &=n\frac{M}{N}\frac{MNn-MN-Nn+N^2-Mnn+Mn}{N(N-1)}\\
                         &=n\frac{M}{N}\frac{N^2-MN-Nn+Mn}{N(N-1)}\\
                         &=n\frac{M}{N}\frac{(N-M)(N-n)}{N(N-1)}\\
                         &=n\frac{M}{N}\frac{N-M}{N}\frac{N-n}{N-1}= n\frac{M}{N}\left(1-\frac{M}{N}\right )\frac{N-n}{N-1} \tag*{$\blacksquare$}
\end{align*}
$$


</details>
:::
